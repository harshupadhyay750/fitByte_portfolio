FitByte AI

"Your Personal AI-Powered Nutrition & Fitness Companion."

FitByte AI is an Android application engineered with Flutter and Dart, backed by Firebase cloud infrastructure. Built to eliminate the friction of health tracking, FitByte AI equips users with real-time calorie calculations, macronutrient balance monitoring, personalized vegetarian diet planning, and cloud-synced profile management in a fluid, dark-themed mobile experience.

User Authentication with Firebase

Personalized Diet Planning

Diet Strategy

Daily Calorie Tracking

Real-Time Math

Step-by-Step Guide

How to Install FitByte AI

Follow these simple steps to install the FitByte AI APK directly on your Android device.

1

Click Download APK

Tap the Download APK button above or click the direct release link to download the FitByte.apk file (~29 MB).

2

Wait for Download

Allow your mobile browser or download manager to finish saving the APK package to your Android device.

3

Open the APK File

Tap the download completion notification or navigate to your device's Downloads folder and tap FitByte.apk.

4

Allow Permission

If Android displays an "Unknown Sources" prompt, tap Settings and toggle Allow from this source to proceed.

5

Tap Install

Confirm the package prompt by tapping Install. Android will verify and install FitByte AI in seconds.

6

Open & Enjoy

Tap Open to launch FitByte AI, sign in with your account, and start managing your nutrition and fitness goals!

Android Security Notice

Because FitByte AI is distributed directly via GitHub Releases as a standalone APK package rather than through the Google Play Store, Android and Google Play Protect may display a routine warning (such as "File might be harmful" or "Install unknown apps"). This is standard Android security protocol for any third-party APK installation. The application is completely clean, safe, and built directly from the verified open-source repository.
